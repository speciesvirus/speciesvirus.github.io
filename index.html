Your screen. Your choice.

Caster helps you find videos and play them on your TV. This page explains what stays on your device, which outside services you use, and how optional Pro access works.

No account. No Caster server.

You do not need a Caster account. Caster does not operate a server that receives your videos or browsing history. Casting connects your phone, the website providing a video, and your chosen receiver. Websites, receivers and your operating system have their own privacy practices.

For privacy questions, contact developer@unbok.com.

Information on your device

History, favorites, searches, site shortcuts, playback positions, settings, open tabs and temporary video copies are stored on your device. You can remove entries or clear saved data in Caster Settings. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup according to your backup settings.

Free keeps up to 20 recent entries, 3 favorites, 2 custom shortcuts, and up to 500 MB of temporary video copies for 7 days. Pro provides unlimited history, favorites and shortcuts, and up to 2 GB of temporary copies for 30 days. Earlier saved entries are preserved when limits change. Storage above Free receives a seven-day grace period when Pro ends before normal cleanup resumes.

Private tabs

Private tabs do not add searches or visited videos to Caster history. They use separate cookies and website storage, which are erased when the last private tab closes. Open private-tab addresses and titles may be restored on this device until you close them. Pro can protect access using Face ID, fingerprint or your phone's screen lock. An existing lock stays protected when Pro ends.

Private mode does not hide traffic from the websites you visit, your network provider or the receiver you cast to. Caster does not request or display its own ads in private tabs.

Ads in Free

The Free version uses Google AdMob for small native ad cards on Cast home, Browser start and Library. Ads are not requested or shown during casting or playback, in the player or volume panel, or in private tabs. Pro removes Caster ads. There are no automatic full-screen ads.

You can choose to watch a rewarded ad for 24 hours of Pro on this installation. Only completing the rewarded ad unlocks the pass. It does not start a subscription or charge you.

AdMob may process IP addresses (which can estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and protect its services. Caster does not add your video titles, browsing history or private-tab content to ad requests. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent and tracking choices

Where required, Google's consent form appears before ads can be requested. You can reopen Ad privacy choices in Caster Settings when that option is required for your region. On iPhone, you can decline tracking and change your choice in system Settings. Declining tracking keeps all Free features available.

When iPhone tracking permission is declined, Caster requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized or limited ads can still process data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to consent requirements. Google processes data under its own policy.

Search, websites and voice

Opening a website, video, shortcut icon or page preview contacts that website. Searches are sent to your search provider. Optional Google autocomplete sends the search text you type to Google; you can turn it off in Settings, and it is disabled in private tabs.

Voice search uses your phone's speech-recognition service only when you tap the microphone and grant permission. The service may process audio remotely. Caster shows the recognized words for you to review before searching. It does not store voice recordings.

Local-network access discovers and connects to receivers. File and photo access lets you select media to play. Subtitle files are served temporarily from the phone to a Google Cast receiver on your local network; Caster does not upload them to a cloud service.

Subscriptions and billing

Caster Pro is optional and automatically renews through Apple App Store or Google Play. The purchase screen shows the store's localized price, billing period and any eligible trial. Apple or Google handles payment; Caster does not receive your card details.

Eligible offers include a seven-day free trial. After a trial, the displayed subscription price is charged unless you cancel before renewal. You can manage or cancel through your store's subscription settings. Deleting Caster does not cancel a subscription. Restore purchases checks the subscription associated with your current store account.

A rewarded 24-hour pass is local to this installation, does not automatically renew, and cannot be transferred or restored through store purchases. Video formats, subtitle support and filtering depend on your website, phone and receiver. Queues and sleep timers require Caster to stay running and connected.

For the iOS app, Apple's standard license agreement applies.

Your choices and changes

You can clear Caster's stored history and searches, close private tabs, remove temporary media, change permissions in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. To manage information held by Google or Apple, use their privacy and account controls.

We may update this policy when Caster's features or service providers change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
